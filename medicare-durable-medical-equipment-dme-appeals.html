Filing a Medicare DME Appeal

You may file a reconsideration request with Maximus if your claim for Medicare DME items or services was denied. Your reconsideration request will be handled by Maximus. The DME QIC jurisdiction encompasses all states.

After a DME Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) has made a redetermination (first level appeal) for Medicare items or services, a reconsideration (second level appeal request) may be filed as follows:

How to File An Appeal

The request must be filed with Maximus and can be submitted via the following methods:

  • QIC Appeals Portal at https://qicappeals.cms.gov (opens in new tab)
  • In writing to the Maximus address indicated on the notice of redetermination. Part B DME standard reconsideration requests should be mailed to: Maximus DME QIC 3750 Monroe Ave Suite 777 Pittsford, NY 14534-1302
  • Via secure fax to 585-869-3314

The request must be in writing. The request should be submitted via a standard CMS form (opens in new tab), or alternatively, the request must contain:

  • The beneficiary’s name
  • Medicare Beneficiary Identifier or Medicare health insurance claim number
  • The specific service(s) and item(s) for which the reconsideration is requested and the specific date(s) of service
  • The name of the party or the representative/billing agency (opens in new tab) of the party
  • The name of the contractor that made the redetermination

Submitting Personal or Confidential Information

Any document that contains the identity of a beneficiary or a provider is confidential. For this reason, Maximus cannot accept confidential information sent via email transmission. Submit confidential information to Maximus in hard copy by US mail, commercial delivery service or via secure fax to 585-869-3314.

Sending an Email

Electronic mail is not necessarily secure against interception. If your communication is very sensitive or includes personal information, please send it by postal mail or fax (585-869-3314) instead. If you still wish to email us, please send to DMEInfo@maximus.com.
